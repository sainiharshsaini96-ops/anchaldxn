Frequently Asked Questions

RG (Reishi Gano) is derived from the mature 90-day fruit body of Ganoderma, known for its dense triterpenoid profile, while GL (Ganocelium) is harvested at 14 days from the delicate mycelium threads rich in organic germanium and natural enzyme precursors.

In traditional usage guidelines, they are recommended as complementary pairs to provide balanced holistic support. However, each formulation can also be consumed independently based on personal health goals or practitioner advice.
